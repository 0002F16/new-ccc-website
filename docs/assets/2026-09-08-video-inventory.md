owner's desired experienced-professional audience."* Reusing them wholesale would
reimport the problem the new page exists to fix.

### Decision, 8 September 2026

Use only the videos that match the experienced ICP. **Filming new matched cases is
a launch blocker, not a later phase.** Target four to five, drawn from clients with
3+ years of relevant experience in one of the two situations.

### Required before any video is published

Per video, all seven:

1. **Consent to reuse.** Consent to being filmed for the old site is not consent to
   appear on a new commercial page. Obtain it in writing, specific to this page.
2. **Service actually received** — DFY or coaching. The research pack's central
   unknown. A coaching testimonial presented as evidence of the done-for-you service
   is a claim-rule breach however good the video is.
3. **Relevant experience** at the time of engagement, in years.
4. **Starting situation** — which of the two the page addresses.
5. **Exact milestone** — interview invitation, signed offer, or confirmed job start.
   The site's current wording does not distinguish these reliably.
6. **Absolute date**, not a relative one.
7. **Specific work performed** by CCC, for the case card's "work done" field.

### Two specific problems in the existing set

**Outcome stacking.** `Gh50BMbTUoM` ("4 Tier-1 Offers") and `rfgxaKpNE5k`
("Multi-Offers") both aggregate outcomes. The claim rules require one precise
milestone per case. Relabel to a single stated milestone or do not use.

**Employer names as proof.** Four carry employer names in the strap-line — US Bank,
JP Morgan, Goldman Sachs, Maersk, Citi, DHL, Linklaters, Nomagic, Chisage. The
claim rules permit an employer name only to locate a specific verified example, and
forbid a logo wall without context. Each name must attach to a verified case or come
off.

**One useful side effect.** `otKB4VQQVHU` labels P06 as **Data Analyst**, which is
one side of the Business Development / Data Analyst conflict the research pack flags.
It is evidence toward reconciliation, not the reconciliation itself.

## Founder channel

`youtube.com/@pointofaziz` — "Aziz Khaitov", 436 subscribers, **191 videos**.
Bio: *"Founder Capital Career Club. I help internationals land jobs in Poland."*
Links to `free.capitalcareerclub.com`.

**Two uses on the new page.**

1. **Woven-in founder presence.** Four of the five critiques independently found the
   page has no human presence. 191 videos means there is no shortage of footage or
   stills for a poster frame, a signed statement, and a face in the reframe section.
2. **The free-resources route.** The secondary path for people outside the paid
   offer, per the strategy brief. `free.capitalcareerclub.com` is the existing
   destination.

**Do not carry the channel bio's claims onto the page.** It states placement totals
and an annual client-earnings figure. Both are forbidden by the claim rules, and the
two surfaces disagree with each other on the second figure.

## No VSL was found

The spec assumes a video sales letter exists and lists its audit as a blocker. The
live site contains **no VSL** — only the nine testimonials and one decorative muted
autoplay loop used as a background.

So the video slot in the new architecture has no asset behind it. Either the VSL
exists somewhere not yet supplied, or it has to be made. Until one exists, the video
section is a designed slot with nothing in it, and the page must genuinely work
without it — which the architecture already requires.

**Open:** does a VSL exist off-site? If not, commission one, scripted against the
offer letter and the claim rules rather than against the current homepage's copy.
