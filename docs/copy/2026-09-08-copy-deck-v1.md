# MOVEMENT II — WHY IT HAS NOT WORKED

## 4. Three bottlenecks

**Eyebrow**
> WHY THE EFFORT IS NOT CONVERTING

**h2**
> Most stalled searches are stuck on one of three things

**Lede**
> Not all three, and not the same one for everyone. Working out which one is yours
> is the first thing the fit call does.

### Bottleneck 01 — volume
*Situation marker:* BOTH SITUATIONS
> A real search runs on repetition. Ten or more properly targeted applications a
> week, every week, each one followed up. Almost nobody sustains that alongside a
> job, and almost nobody sustains it through month four of unemployment either. The
> search does not fail on any one application. It fails on the weeks that get
> skipped.

### Bottleneck 02 — reach
*Situation marker:* MOSTLY STALLED SEARCH
> The roles you can see are the roles everyone can see. An advertised job with
> three hundred applicants is a queue, and the CV is your only way through it.
> Nothing about applying harder changes the shape of that queue.

*Claim note:* deliberately does not assert that most senior roles are filled before
being advertised. That is in the offer letter and is unevidenced. This states what
is observable about an advertised role instead.

### Bottleneck 03 — the interview itself
*Situation marker:* BOTH SITUATIONS
> Some people are getting interviews and losing them. Walking in with a company
> page skimmed that morning and no idea who is across the table is not preparation,
> and the difference between the candidate who knows the panel and the candidate
> who does not is visible in the first ten minutes.

*Claim note:* 43 of 145 respondents reported interviews without success. This
bottleneck exists so that reader has an entry point on the page.
