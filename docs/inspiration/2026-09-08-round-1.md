## 1. jamessmith.business — business mentorship

**Type:** long-form sales page, 18,785px tall. No video hero.

**Structure:** urgency bar (countdown, sale) → eyebrow + social-proof pill
("Join 12,000+…") → headline → subhead → founder video → testimonial row with
outcome labels → founder credibility stats (5M followers, 4X 7-figure
businesses) → three-part founder story, numbered → what's included → team
profiles with named specialisms → weekly live schedule → repeated CTA
throughout, same words every time ("Join Today & Save $1000").

**Tokens:** Neue Montreal throughout, one family, no serif. Near-black
`#0E0F12` page, `#1C1D22` cards, electric blue `#1F58D0` for every CTA. Radius
16px dominant (105 uses), buttons 15px. Section padding 85–120px. Container
1360px with an 883px text column. h2 48px/48px w700; body 16px w500. Buttons
20px/80px padding, w700, no letterspacing.

**Worth stealing:** the same CTA words repeated ~8 times; the team section that
names individuals and what each one does; outcome-labelled testimonials
("From 5 → 40 clients") rather than free-floating praise; one typeface,
one accent colour, zero decoration.

**Not for us:** countdown/scarcity bar; income figures as proof.

## 2. start.imperiumacquisition.com — free AI course funnel

**Type:** VSL funnel, 8,420px. Shortest of the five.

**Structure:** eyebrow → headline naming a price anchor ("I used to charge
$3,000… today free") → subhead telling you exactly what the video does →
three reassurance chips → video → CTA + star rating → "Who am I & why is this
free" (handles the obvious suspicion head-on) → itemised value stack with

**Tokens:** light and warm. White page, body text `#4A443F` (warm grey, not
black), headings `#1A1410`. Orange `#EE6A1A` for every CTA. Pastel tint blocks
break up sections: mint `#D9FFF5`, lime `#EAF6D8`, `#E4FF9C`. Inter + Archivo,

**Worth stealing:** answering the reader's suspicion in its own titled section;
warm off-black text instead of pure black; tinted section blocks as rhythm
without new colours; the tight 760px reading column; negative tracking on
large headings.

**Not for us:** value-stack pricing theatre; "was $3,000, now free".

## Pattern across all five

**Structural conventions, consistent:**

1. Audience named in an eyebrow above the headline, before anything is sold
2. One CTA, same words, repeated every 1.5–2 screens
3. Objections pre-empted as chips or a titled section, early, before proof
4. Founder credibility as a distinct block — who is this person, why them
5. The offer itemised rather than described in prose
6. Long pages — three of the five run 8,000–19,000px
7. Single typeface family, single accent colour, near-zero decoration

**Surface conventions, split two ways:** dark and high-contrast (1, 3, 5) or
warm light with pastel section blocks (2). No middle ground in this set.

**The problem this set creates.** All five run on quantified outcome proof —
income figures, tracked sales, follower counts. That is the engine underneath
their structure. CCC's claim rules forbid the equivalent: no placement rates,
no salary-increase promises, no guaranteed timelines. So the structure is
borrowable and the persuasion engine is not. Every slot these pages fill with
a number, CCC has to fill with something else: named client cases with precise
outcome labels, visible qualification criteria, and a full explanation of the
work performed. That substitution is the central design problem of this page.
